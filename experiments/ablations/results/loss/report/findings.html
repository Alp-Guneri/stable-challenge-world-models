Ablation findings: the loss

results/loss · 23 Nov 2026 · CNN encoder + MLP predictor (the reference architecture) with 9 loss presets · 3 seeds × 32,768 steps each, float32, batch 128 · held-out metrics on 1,332 windows from the 333 reserved episodes. ref, lewm and the anchors are reused from results/main.

Complete: 9 of 9 presets, 3 seeds each. All numbers are the team's local proxies on held-out training-corpus episodes, not the hidden leaderboard evaluation.
In short
  • InfoNCE is the term that matters. Removing it from the reference loss drops held-out top-1 from 73.0% to 38.8%. Adding it to the LeWM loss raises it from 57.7% to 74.9%, slightly above the reference loss.
  • InfoNCE is not under-tuned. Halving or doubling its weight, or halving or doubling its temperature, moves top-1 by at most 1.2 points (72.3–74.2%).
  • The within-clip VCReg term is not needed for retrieval. Without it top-1 is 74.6% and prediction error is lower, but one seed loses some motion R².
  • The loss has little headroom left. The best presets beat ref by 1–2 points of top-1 and by at most 0.006 in blend. The main and encoder suites move top-1 by 25–50 points.

Held-out metrics, mean over 3 seeds. Without InfoNCE the reference loss loses 34 points of top-1, a quarter of its motion R² and most of its use of the actions. Adding InfoNCE to LeWM's MSE + SIGReg gains 17 points and ends 2 points above ref, with the best action ranking of any preset. Its prediction error is the highest, though (nMSE 0.118), as LeWM's MSE weight is 25× lower.

Without InfoNCE the model learns a good MSE but not distinct predictions

ref_nonce reaches the lowest training MSE of any preset (0.015 vs 0.033 for ref), and its embeddings are not collapsed (effective rank ≈ 91, close to ref's ≈ 100). But its in-batch retrieval stops at 0.50 within 3,000 steps and stays there for all three seeds. Predictions land close to their targets on average but cannot be told apart from other windows' targets. The same 0.50 plateau appears in the stuck seeds of cnn-ar-ref (main) and vitstack-mlp-ref (encoder suite). Its cause is still unknown.

LeWM's loss starts with a collapse phase; InfoNCE shortens it

Both SIGReg presets spend their first steps collapsed (effective rank 1, MSE near 10−4): lewm_nce until about step 2,000 and lewm until about step 2,500–3,000. After that, lewm_nce reaches the ref curve by about step 10,000, while lewm is still improving at the end. Every preset with the reference loss's MSE weight and VCReg leaves collapse within a few hundred steps.

Held-out top-1. Both knobs point the same way: a stronger, sharper InfoNCE helps slightly. The best of the four, twice the weight, gains 1.2 points, beyond the seed spread but small. Blend moves by at most 0.006, and motion R² and normalized MSE barely change (0.938–0.948 and 0.065–0.069).

4. VCReg within the clip is not needed for retrieval

ref_novcregt drops the term that spreads the three frames of one clip apart. Top-1 rises slightly (74.6% ± 1.4, seeds 74.1 / 73.6 / 76.3%) and normalized MSE is the lowest of all presets (0.047 vs 0.067). The cost shows up in the seed spread: the best seed's motion R² falls to 0.866 (the other two are 0.943–0.949), and embeddings use fewer directions (effective rank 66–74 vs ≈ 100). The term is not what makes ref work, but it may keep the embedding's motion information stable.

5. The other metrics

  • Top-1 measures dynamics, not episode recognition: retrieval from the encoder alone, with no prediction, is 1.0–1.6% for every preset.
  • Every preset produces a valid submission (test_submission.py passed 27/27). All presets train in the same time (75–85 min per seed) and give the same 5.7 MiB ONNX file, so the loss is free to choose.
  • Normalized MSE does not track top-1: the lowest values belong to ref_novcregt (0.047) and ref_nonce (0.059), one of the best and the worst preset for retrieval.

6. Caveats

  • Only the CNN + MLP architecture was tested. In main the ViT barely reacted to the loss, so these gains may not transfer.
  • Each knob was moved one at a time from ref; combinations (e.g. twice the InfoNCE weight without VCReg within the clip) were not run.
  • Three seeds give a spread, not a confidence interval. Differences of about 1 point are near the seed spread of ref_novcregt and lewm.
  • Local proxies only; the hidden evaluation may weigh retrieval, motion and MSE differently.

7. What this suggests

  • Keep InfoNCE, at about its current setting. It accounts for nearly all of ref's advantage, and tuning it is worth at most 1–2 points.
  • If one more loss run is worth doing, it is ref with twice the InfoNCE weight (and possibly τ = 0.05) on the final architecture. Treat VCReg within the clip as optional and check motion R² if it is dropped.
  • Spend the remaining effort on the encoder, where the differences are 25–50 points (see results/encoder/report/findings.html).
  • The 0.50 in-batch plateau now appears in three suites. It may be worth understanding before trying other unstable settings.
